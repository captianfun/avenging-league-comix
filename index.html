Avenging League Comix

Hello! Welcome to Avenging League Comix.

This whole thing started one day when I decided to make my own comic with my best freind. Then I made more and more, and created a universe.

These comics were sold at a comic sale in Vancouver. After that, I had other interests. Then I decided to stop...

Until Stickman. That was just a short bit of comic with no evident plotline and no words at all. But I got back into the comic-making biz, and I went back to Avenging League.

I hope you enjoy this website!
